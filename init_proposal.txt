Research Scope — Brainstorm
1. Research Premises
Exact repeated endgame states are rare.
Need to demonstrate that reaching exactly the same late-game hand configuration is a low-probability event.
Team membership does not automatically reduce hidden-hand uncertainty.
Apart from knowing their own hand, a player does not inherently know more about teammates’ remaining cards than opponents’ remaining cards; any additional knowledge must come from inference based on public play history.
2. Fixed Rule Scope
Use the no-tribute version of Da Guai Lu Zi throughout the main study.
Keep one fixed rule set to avoid introducing extra information or material advantages across games.
3. Core Research Question
Compare different strategy families according to how effectively they reduce the coordination problems created by asymmetric information within a team.
The aim is not simply to increase information sharing, but to improve team decision-making under incomplete information.
4. Central Strategic Tension

The key trade-off is:

Reduce harmful information asymmetry within the team, while still exploiting the informational advantage created by each player’s private hand.

In other words:

too little coordination → teammates cannot infer each other’s intentions;
too much signaling / predictability → the team may lose the advantage of private information.
5. Information Leakage to Opponents
Information-reducing strategies may also make the team’s intentions more visible to opponents.
If a team’s intended route becomes too obvious, opponents may anticipate it and deliberately disrupt the sequence.
Therefore, the study should consider both:
coordination benefit within the team
strategic information leakage to the opposing team
6. Strategy Comparison

Rather than searching for a universal “best strategy,” compare strategies on questions such as:

Which strategy most effectively reduces the negative effect of information asymmetry?
Under what conditions does increased coordination improve team outcomes?
At what point does increased predictability begin to benefit the opponent?
7. Control Variables

Assume:

all players are highly experienced;
all understand the rules and strategic principles of Da Guai Lu Zi;
no player is assigned a fixed play style, personal preference, or “school” of strategy.

This allows differences in outcomes to be attributed more cleanly to the strategies being compared rather than player skill.

8. Claim Boundary

Avoid absolute language such as:

“optimal strategy”
“best strategy”
“solves the game”

Prefer:

“performs better under specified conditions”
“reduces coordination loss”
“improves expected team outcome”
“is more robust under the tested information structure”
